Mission 2 – Stats & Inventory

Health, coins, temperatures — numbers that must not crash the game.

1. Briefing video

Kapilearn · Mission 2
Briefing notes

Variables are labelled boxes. C# wants to know what’s inside: whole numbers, decimals, true/false, or text. TryParse is your crash helmet for user input.

Use decimal for money. double is fine for science — less fine for “why is my shop 0.01p short?”

📖

1. Briefing video

Kapilearn · Mission 3
Briefing notes

% means remainder, not percent. 10 / 4 is 2 when both are ints — C# ate the 0.5. Rude, but consistent. Use brackets when precedence gets spicy.

📖

2. Loadout

Precedence: 2 + 3 * 4 = 14. Brackets if unsure.

Common mistake
Expecting 10 / 4 to be 2.5. Write 10.0 / 4 when you need the fraction.
